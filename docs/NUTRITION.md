# Nutrition rules and numerical engine

39 canonical nutrients defined in data/nutrients.json and schemas/nutrients.schema.json. Empty library data/foods.csv and foods.json. Keep all 39 keys on every reference with number or null. Zero is a measured/verified absence, not default. Optional additional `added_sugar_g` lives separately only if known; total sugar is not added sugar. Salt equivalent g = sodium_mg * 2.5 / 1000, not a separate intake to sum. Use proper vitamin A/Folate equivalence (RAE/DFE when source and reference compatible), preserve source unit and conversion. Cholesterol, fatty acid subtype, mineral and caffeine units distinct. Avoid adding subtypes to parent macros. Water includes defined logged water/hydration separately from food water; distinguish beverages vs total water and don't double count. Never infer dose from product name.

Food research priority: authoritative food composition databases (USDA FoodData Central, official local composition tables if actually accessible), manufacturer labels, credible published recipes/ingredient references, then explicitly uncertain recipe estimate. For Iranian foods compare matching preparation, cooked/raw, serving mass, fat and recipe; convert to same basis before comparison. Do not assume higher kcal means higher fat; do not blindly average incomparable values. Recipe calculation uses ingredient amount * nutrients per basis, sum and divide by final edible yield, account for water/oil changes and verified retention factors, do not invent missing ingredient nutrients. Sources per field or source group, actual fetched URL, access date, basis, method, confidence, range/assumptions. If search unavailable or evidence inadequate, null and deferred status; no fake source. Store variant-specific portion estimates separately, with bounds and density as necessary. For unknown portion, ask API only for that portion, not whole food. If high-impact ambiguity (e.g. one un-sized package, conflicting allergens or supplement dose), use review; otherwise store best estimate + assumptions. Model must never infer medical allergies from notes or recommend an allergen.

REE Mifflin–St Jeor adult approximation: 10*kg + 6.25*cm - 5*age + 5 (male reference) or -161 (female reference). TDEE = REE * rough activity factor (1.2/1.375/1.55/1.725/1.9). Factors are planning assumptions, not measured data. If missing reference variable, no exact result; optionally display labeled range. Not minimum calorie intake. Actual Health resting+active is separate, do not sum with TDEE. Do not prescribe extreme restriction or classify caloric deficit as moral success.

Weekly totals: sum known per item; daily coverage includes logged days, parsed notes, each nutrient's known item count/known mass where comparable, and Health valid days. No log does not mean no food. Average denominator explicit, reference period matched to days with usable observations. Report missing nutrient data alongside known subtotal. For reference comparison use versioned authoritative DRI age/reference sex/life stage RDA or AI; UL and other limits have different semantics, sometimes only supplements. No clinical deficiency from intake; avoid false claims based on low coverage. If reference absent, do not calculate percentage. Percentages not clamped in text; chart may cap with overflow label. Data quality conditions configurable only with owner agreement. Compare monthly on paired days with sufficiently complete intake and expenditure; show coverage and avoid extrapolation by default. Net = intake - expenditure; rough weight delta = net / approved kcal_per_kg. User's proposed 2600 coefficient unverified; conventional ~7700 simplified and not predictive of exact weight. Ask before selection; never use custom unvalidated value for diet advice. Water retention, measurement noise, adaptation, etc. explain divergence without diagnosis. Sources in SOURCES.md.

For a supplement label, preserve each original per-serving value and the consumed serving count. Convert compatible g, mg and µg mass units for nutrient totals. Named blends such as B complex are retained as label entries, with no inferred allocation to individual nutrients.
