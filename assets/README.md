## 2026-11-26 artwork

The built-in ImageGen tool created [tea](illustrations/tea.jpg), [alcohol-free beer](illustrations/alcohol-free-beer.jpg), and [journal paper](illustrations/journal-paper.jpg). The original generated compositions were resized and encoded as JPEG for the app.

### tea

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Calio matching drink artwork. Create a single photo-real watercolor still life of Persian black tea in a transparent small tulip-shaped tea glass on a muted cream ceramic saucer. Match the reference drink artwork's warm sepia/cream Japandi palette, paper texture, soft ambient light, front three-quarter camera, and centered composition. Solid warm ivory rice-paper backdrop, smooth blank edges, generous breathing room, main object readable at 64px, square crop. Very subtle shadow, no text or labels, no lemon or mint. The supplied existing coffee artwork is a style reference only.

### alcohol-free-beer

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Calio matching drink artwork. Create a single low-key photo-real watercolor still life of an unbranded clear straight-sided tumbler holding pale amber alcohol-free malt beer, distinguished from tea by one low cream foam head and subtle small bubbles. Match the existing Calio water and coffee palette of muted tan, paper ivory, natural soft light and soft watercolor texture. Framing centered at small app card size, square composition, consistent front three-quarter eye level, blank warm cream paper edges, very subtle shadow, no bottle, people, words or logos. The supplied water glass is a style reference only.

### journal-paper

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: faint background within an existing journal entry form. Create a wide horizontal real rice-paper textured artwork with pale ivory paper and an understated walnut-gray botanical branch shadow from the far corner. At least two thirds of the canvas almost blank, warm soft natural light, like a Japanese paper-screen shadow. Branch shadow diffuse, very low contrast, light ochre accents, no text, letters, frames or interface. Designed as a faded background behind a form where its controls remain legible. The supplied Calio ginkgo is a palette and medium reference only.
